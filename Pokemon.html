Founder Profile: Kevin Li

Boston-born founder of the Pokémon-native collector economy.

Kevin Li is the visionary behind KevinsPokeCenter — a first-of-its-kind collector command center built for the next generation of serious Pokémon holders, grail hunters, vault builders, and live-market participants.

“Collecting is not only ownership. It is identity, memory, strategy, and culture.”

— Kevin Li, Founder of KevinsPokeCenter

Raised in Boston, Kevin grew up with the same spark that defines millions of Pokémon fans around the world: the thrill of discovery, the chase for rare cards, the emotional pull of favorite characters, and the belief that collecting is more than a childhood hobby.

Where most people saw binders, booster packs, and nostalgia, Kevin saw something larger: an economy. He recognized that Pokémon collecting had evolved into a global marketplace of grails, slabs, sealed products, auctions, vaults, reputations, watchlists, liquidity, and live demand.

Yet the experience still felt fragmented. Collectors were jumping between marketplaces, auction rooms, price charts, grading companies, social feeds, and private groups just to understand what was happening. Kevin believed Pokémon collectors deserved better.

That belief became KevinsPokeCenter: a premium collector operating system where collectors can track Pokémon-specific markets, follow elite holders, monitor demand, join live auction rooms, analyze vault portfolios, and experience collecting with the intensity of a financial trading desk and the soul of a childhood dream.

Kevin’s innovation is connecting emotion with infrastructure. A Charizard is not just a listing. A Rayquaza is not just a slab. A Pikachu trophy card is not just an asset. Each item carries history, rarity, status, and story.
